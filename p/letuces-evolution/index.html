Crops evolution

I started my seedlings on December 14th 2020. In December th I planted the seedlings in mineral cubes and put them under LED light. One weed later December 29th, I put the cubes in the plant baskets and placed them in my containers.You can see the evolutions on the pictures below.

December 14, 2020

I tried 4 kinds of letuces but the one presented here are the “Grosse Blonde Paresseuse”. It was that lettuce that performed the most, with the less issues and the one that grew faster.
